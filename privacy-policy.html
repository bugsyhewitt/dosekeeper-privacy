Privacy Policy

DoseKeeper — Medication Reminder

Last updated: September 25, 2026

DoseKeeper collects no data. None.

DoseKeeper collects no personal data, has no accounts, and sends nothing off your device. Every medication, schedule, and dose record you enter is stored only in this app's local storage on your phone — never on a server, never synced, never analyzed. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no trackers.

What's stored, and where

DoseKeeper stores whatever you enter: medication names, doses, schedules, notes, an optional pill photo, and your dose history (taken/skipped/snoozed timestamps) and refill counts. All of it lives in a local, on-device database. None of it is transmitted anywhere by the app. Uninstalling the app deletes it, along with everything else on your device the app wrote.

Data export is yours to control

The app's CSV/PDF export (Settings → Export) is generated entirely on-device and handed to whatever app or service you choose from the system share sheet (email, another app, a cloud drive you already use, etc.). DoseKeeper itself never uploads or transmits the export — where it goes after you tap "share" is entirely your choice and outside this policy's scope.

Network

The core app works fully offline and does not request internet access for its reminder, scheduling, history, or export features. There are no in-app purchases: everything in DoseKeeper is free. The only link out of the app is the optional Donate row in Settings, which opens a Stripe donation page in your browser (subject to Stripe's own privacy policy — we never see your payment details). Donating unlocks nothing, and the app never asks.

Permissions, in plain terms

  • Exact alarms / full-screen alerts — so a dose reminder fires at the exact time you set and can wake the screen, even if the phone has been idle. Used only for the reminders you schedule.
  • Notifications — to show the reminder itself and its Taken/Snooze/Skip actions.
  • Boot / time-change receivers — to re-arm your reminders after a restart or a clock change. No data leaves the device to do this.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit information about you.

Not a medical device

DoseKeeper is a reminder, logging, and export tool. It is not medical advice and does not check for drug interactions. Always consult your doctor or pharmacist before starting, stopping, or changing a medication.

Children's privacy

The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.
